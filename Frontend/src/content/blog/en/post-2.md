    "A donor pool is only as valuable as the trust it protects. The moment an organization sends messages to people who never agreed to receive them, that trust erodes. Changia treats consent as a first-class record, not a checkbox afterthought.",
    "In the Changia Donor Pool, every contact carries a consent status, a preferred channel and a contact history. Phone numbers are normalized and deduplicated, so the same person is never counted twice. When a name or email is unknown, the donor can still be stored with limited data.",
    "Bulk outreach — SMS, WhatsApp or email — draws only from consented contacts. The system offers opt-out and suppression management for each channel, and an import screen shows rejected records, duplicates and the number of valid contacts before a single message is sent.",
    "A separate point matters for push donations: consent to marketing is distinct from consent to receive a one-time, agreed payment request. A Campaign  manager records each agreement, the amount and the outcome, and rate limits prevent repeated or unwanted requests.",
