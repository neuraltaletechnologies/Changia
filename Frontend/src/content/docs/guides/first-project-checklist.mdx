Embarking on a new project can be exciting and challenging in equal measure. With the right preparation and guidance, your venture is poised for success. The Changia  First Project Checklist is designed to provide a clear and structured approach to ensure you're well-prepared every step of the way.

## Project Preparation

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Scope and Objectives">
    - Ensure clarity of the project's scope, deliverables, and desired outcomes
    - Define clear objectives and success criteria for project completion
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tools and Equipment">
    - Make a list of all required Changia  tools and equipment
    - Verify inventory availability and condition before starting
  </Card>
  <Card title="Arranging Services">
    - If additional expertise or manpower is needed, arrange for Changia  construction services in advance
    - Clarify service level agreements and timelines with your Changia  representative
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Project Execution
### Initial Phase
<Steps>
1. Set up the project site according to Changia  setup guidelines

2. Conduct an initial user meeting to align on project goals and Changia  methodologies

3. Establish project checkpoints and milestones for regular assessment
</Steps>
